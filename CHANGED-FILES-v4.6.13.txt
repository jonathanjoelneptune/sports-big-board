ROOT CAUSE
The operator playlist crawler selected candidate events from a video's parsed or
published date before the v4.6.12 alias-aware matcher ran. LLWS therefore ingested
74 source assets but could leave them all orphaned even when titles/aliases proved
the correct game.

BEHAVIOR
1. Built-in league playlist behavior is unchanged.
2. SPECIAL_EVENT playlist media is title-rule filtered first.
3. Each eligible asset is compared against the full competition schedule.
4. Actual participant names plus imported aliases/groups/regions/abbreviations are
   valid matchup evidence.
5. If the same pair plays more than once, provider ID, title game number, explicit
   title date, and bounded publication proximity are deterministic tie-breakers.
6. Ambiguous repeated matchups remain orphaned; v4.6.13 never guesses.
7. Existing downloaded operator-playlist source media is re-associated at startup
   without redownloading it.
8. Future special-event crawls store both internal operator and YouTube playlist IDs.
9. Special-event Statistics WITH MEDIA is overwritten with durable playable
   EVENT_MEDIA truth, matching what the ribbon can actually play.
10. REPROCESS MEDIA automatically uses the new tournament-wide crawler.
