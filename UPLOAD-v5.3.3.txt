PRIMARY USER-VISIBLE CHANGES
- TEAM BROWSE / PLAYER BROWSE is visually joined to the selected league as a true submenu rather than a disconnected top-level button.
- The Browse popover is hosted inside the fullscreenable app shell and repositions on fullscreen changes, so it remains usable in browser fullscreen.
- The curated media ribbon exists only while a curated Team / Player / Ranked / Seeded / All Highlights view is active.
- The old DAY back button is replaced by a clear EXIT action on the right side of the curated toolbar.
- Curated timeline matchup, score, date, and media-tier typography is larger and easier to scan.
- Starting a curated competition that has no Game Center implementation (for example US Open tennis) clears/hides stale Game Center content from the previously watched sport while preserving Coming Up.
- Existing chronological queue behavior is unchanged: clicking a card starts there and continues through every older result in the current curated library.

FUNCTIONAL BOUNDARIES
- No second playback owner.
- No new PROGRAM model.
- No polling loop or continuous browse observer.
- Score-ribbon interrupt/resume behavior remains authoritative.
- Historical catalog and verified media remain the Browse data source.
