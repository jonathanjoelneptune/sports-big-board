A. World Cup stale-MLB regression

B. World Cup / US Open League View
   - Enter FIFA WC and open League View before selecting a team.
   - Expected: tournament group information when source group metadata exists plus a populated knockout-round view from the full event library.
   - Enter US OPEN and open League View.
   - Expected: Round 1/2/etc., quarterfinal, semifinal, and final groupings based on the complete event library rather than a blank standings panel.

C. League navigation
   - Click MLB, NFL, NBA, NHL, EPL, MLS, or NCAAF.
   - Expected: TODAY | ALL | TEAM BROWSE appears inline and League View switches immediately to that league even before a video is selected.
   - TODAY returns to today's league-filtered score ribbon.
   - ALL opens the full historical highlight rail for that league.
   - Selecting a video makes the playing video's league authoritative again.

D. Soccer form
   - Open EPL and MLS League View.
   - Expected: each team has five form slots; W is green, draw is a gray dash, L is red, and unplayed slots remain hollow.
   - Standings remain compact and readable.

E. NHL width / symmetry
   - Open NHL League View.
   - Expected: Eastern and Western headings remain aligned, shorter paired sections retain blank row spacing, and W-L-OT records no longer wrap with duplicate PTS text.

F. Refresh stability
   - Leave League View open while scores/SelectedEvent/title state changes.
   - Expected: correct standings remain visible. No repeated one-second loading spinner/fetch loop.
