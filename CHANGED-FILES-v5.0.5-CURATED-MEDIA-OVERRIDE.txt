Architecture
------------
- New architecture/curated-media-overrides.js registry.
- Curated entries are data-driven and reusable for future event corrections.
- Matching uses canonical provider IDs first, with date/team fingerprint fallback.
- Curated media is inserted at the front of the event Media Plan and becomes the
  editorial primary while retaining normal v5 quarantine, watchdog, recovery,
  Game Center ownership and same-event fallback behavior.
- app.js contains no USC/event-specific conditional or YouTube ID.
- The exact USC URL/ID exists only in the curated registry and regression test.

Certification
-------------
- Comprehensive Certification schema 3.5.
- New CURATED MEDIA REGRESSION section.
- Every registry entry marked regressionFixture is actively selected from the real
  score ribbon, must choose its configured physical media, must advance the media
  clock, must keep Game Center ownership, must preserve the v5 invariant, and must
  not trigger a global engine reset.
- The general randomized playback matrix remains game-agnostic.
