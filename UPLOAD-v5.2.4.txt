What changed
------------
1. KEY INFO is now SPORTS TICKER. CURRENT NEWS wording is removed at runtime.
2. The ticker browser path reads only a prepared snapshot and never performs OpenAI
   or sports-news discovery in response to a user action.
3. The backend prepares/persists a new edition every 20 minutes (3x/hour), keeps up
   to 150 consequential entries, and serves last-good data if a refresh fails.
4. /api/sports-ticker is canonical. /api/current-news remains a compatibility alias.
5. Ticker categories now cover breaking news, championships, clinches/eliminations,
   playoff movement, rankings, records/record watch, milestones, streaks, major
   results/upsets, transactions, injuries/returns, coaching/discipline, tournaments,
   schedules, draft/recruiting, awards, and retirements.
6. Historical score-date changes do not refresh the Sports Ticker.
7. Date transition ownership is reasserted after legacy v5.2.2 boot wrappers finish,
   preventing the old virtual-ribbon wrapper from independently fetching the selected
   date and prefetching +/-3 dates on every transition.
8. Cold convergence is bounded to four follow-up reads, for a hard maximum of five
   RibbonSnapshot reads per transition instead of an open request fan-out.
9. NCAAF league pill is explicitly vertically centered through the common pill box.
