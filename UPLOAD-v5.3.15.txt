PRIMARY ACCEPTANCE CHECKS
A. FIFA / Special Event Match Center authority
   - Start with a normal MLB Game Center visible.
   - Enter SPECIAL EVENTS > FIFA WC and select Argentina vs Spain, then another World Cup match.
   - Expected: GAME CENTER becomes MATCH CENTER and the right panel always shows the selected World Cup matchup.
   - Standard MLB Game Center content is hidden for the entire Special Event context, even if an old MLB callback arrives late.
   - League View and Settings still replace Match Center when their tabs are selected.

B. Browser-proven Special Event media audit
   - If the first source for a World Cup match is blocked from embedding, the same-match fallback still takes over.
   - The blocked source is persisted as failed for 90 days in browser media-health storage.
   - The source that actually plays is persisted as successful.
   - On the next selection/reload, browser-proven successful media outranks a higher-tier source known to fail.
   - Catalog rows already marked FAILED/BLOCKED/UNPLAYABLE or embedAllowed=false are skipped immediately.

C. Wild Card standings
   - MLB and NFL Wild Card tables show WC GB relative to the final current Wild Card spot, not relative to a division leader.
   - The cutoff team shows an em dash. Teams currently above the cutoff show +N; teams below show N games back.
   - Division leaders remain excluded.

D. EPL / MLS form layout
   - Team/club column is tighter and the five form circles sit directly beside the standings stats rather than against the far edge.
   - W is green, draw is gray with a dash, L is red, and empty early-season slots stay hollow.

E. League subset controls
   - TODAY and ALL use a green treatment so they read as subordinate scope choices under the active league.

Do not upload the outer folder itself into the repository. Upload its contents so VERSION, index.html, ui/, architecture/, sbb/, tests/, tools/, and the other included paths land at their existing repository locations.
