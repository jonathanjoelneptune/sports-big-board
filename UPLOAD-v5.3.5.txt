PRIMARY USER-VISIBLE CHANGES
- Team/Player Browse participant inventories are now persisted in browser storage by league/tournament.
- After a complete league scan succeeds once, reopening Team Browse or Player Browse uses the saved complete list immediately instead of re-reading thousands of audit rows before the menu can populate.
- Saved inventories refresh in the background after six hours so new teams/players with verified highlights are incorporated without blocking the menu.
- On a first-ever uncached league open, today's known participants appear immediately while the complete verified-highlight inventory is built and saved.
- The curated Team/Player Browse ribbon measures the real standard score-ribbon height before switching modes and uses exactly that same vertical slot. Selecting a team/player should therefore no longer move the video/Game Center vertically.
- PLAY ALL and EXIT now appear at both the left and right ends of the curated ribbon. The left pair replaces the old TEAM BROWSE kicker area.
- Historical curated clips now explicitly select their own historical event identity in SelectedEvent/Game Center. A Sep 1 highlight can no longer inherit the Sep 2 Game Center merely because the same two teams are playing again.
- Score-ribbon interrupts remain authoritative: while a user-interrupt game is active, the curated queue does not overwrite its selected Game Center event.

TEAM / PLAYER FOCUS SPORTS TICKER — PHASE 1
- When an actual team/player is selected, the normal generic Sports Ticker is replaced by a contextual focus ribbon for that entity.
- It uses data Sports Big Board already owns: verified-highlight library size, provider record/standing fields when available, recent results, current W/L/T streak, next five scheduled catalog games, and matching current Sports Ticker news.
- Exiting Team/Player Browse restores the normal Sports Ticker.
- This intentionally does NOT make Team Browse dependent on a third-party ranking scrape. A TeamRankings/external-stat adapter can be added next as an enrichment source for deeper league ranks and specialty metrics while keeping the core experience reliable.
