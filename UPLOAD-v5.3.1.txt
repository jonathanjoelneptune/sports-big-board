- Cards no longer display recap counts.
- FULL RECAP / EXTENDED / HIGHLIGHTS / GOLD use their media-tier colors.
- Historical card scores are lazy-enriched from the canonical /api/history/scores endpoint only as cards approach the visible timeline.
- A card click starts that game and automatically queues every older filtered game after it.
- The ambiguous + is replaced by explicit + QUEUE / ✓ QUEUED manual-selection controls.
- The video is viewport-fit whether Game Center is open or collapsed, preventing unnecessary page-height overflow.
