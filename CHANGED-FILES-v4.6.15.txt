ROOT CAUSE
v4.6.14 could prove an LLWS title such as "Ohio vs Alabama | Full Game Highlights"
against the imported participant aliases. HistoryRepository.put_event_media() then
ran the older generic matcher again using only "West Side LL" and
"Phenix City Youth Baseball LL", producing TITLE_TEAM_PAIR_CONFLICT and leaving
the desired videos quarantined.

FIX
1. v4.6.15 decorates only internally-proven SPECIAL_EVENT_TITLE_ALIAS_PAIR results
   with an exact canonical-event proof.
2. Persistence accepts that proof only when league/event/key, both title aliases,
   producer, proof schema, title fingerprint, and confidence all validate.
3. The source asset is moved from QUARANTINED to ASSIGNED only for that exact event.
4. Cross-event assigned assets are never stolen; the new candidate remains quarantined.
5. Ordinary media continues through the original event matcher unchanged.
6. Generic relationship repair is followed by restoration of still-valid internal
   special-event proofs.
7. Existing special-event SOURCE_MEDIA is automatically reassociated at startup;
   no playlist redownload or schedule recreation is required.
