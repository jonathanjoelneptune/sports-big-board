Sports Big Board v5.4.9 Windows Controller Bridge compatibility

No local bridge update is required for v5.4.9.

Keep using the working Sports Big Board v5.4.7 Windows Controller Bridge R1. The bridge protocol, SendInput fullscreen delivery, command-result acknowledgement, and app/video fullscreen whitelist are unchanged.

The repository overlay retains the existing bridge helper source/launcher for repository completeness, but this release does not require recompiling or restarting the bridge on your PC.
